🎯 Nine Card Knock Out - Game Rules

🎯 Knock Out Version

This is a KNOCK OUT version! The goal is to be one of the last 3 players standing. The top 3 players with the highest stacks win prizes!

Elimination: Players are eliminated when they cannot afford to place a bet. Stay alive and accumulate chips to be among the final 3 winners!

🎮 How to Play

Nine Card Knock Out is a casino-style card game where players compete to be the last one standing by selecting cards from A to 9.

🃏 Card Selection

  • Each player selects one card from A, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9
  • Cards are selected in turn order decided by the admin (can be changed in Admin controls)
  • Once a card is selected, it cannot be chosen by another player

🏦 Bank Card

After all players have selected their cards, the bank draws one card (A-10).

💰 Winning Conditions

  • WIN: Your card number is LOWER than the bank's card
  • LOSS: Your card number is the same number as the bank's card
  • REFUND: your card number is HIGHER than the bank's card (you keep your bet minus the jackpot withdraw)
  • BANK 10: If bank draws 10, all players lose except player with card 9

🎰 Payout System

  • Winners: Share the losing player's bet in cascade order (highest number first)
  • Refunds: Keep their original bet minus jackpot contribution
  • Jackpot Contribution: 20% of refunded bets go to the jackpots. Contribution percentage can be changed in admin controls
  • Bank Rake: The casino takes a percentage from the losing player's bet as house commission. Can be changed in admin controls

♥️ Hearts Jackpot

  • How to Win: Draw the same rank AND suit as the bank with ♥️ Hearts!
  • Jackpot Split: Contributions are split 80% to Hearts Jackpot, 20% to Insurance Jackpot. From the Hearts Jackpot portion, a percentage (adjustable in admin) is reserved for Super Jackpot.
  • Payout: When you win the Hearts Jackpot:
    • You get: Your bet back + the entire Hearts Jackpot
    • Other players: Get refund (bet back minus jackpot contribution)
  • Example: If you select the 7♥ and the bank draws 7♥, you win your bet back PLUS the entire Hearts Jackpot! Other players get their refund minus jackpot contribution.

💎 Super Jackpot

  • How to Win: Win the Hearts Jackpot two times in a row! Must be the same player winning both times consecutively.
  • Super Jackpot Contribution: A percentage (default 10%, adjustable in admin controls) of each Hearts Jackpot contribution is reserved for the Super Jackpot, multiplied by the number of simultaneous tables (default 10, adjustable in admin).
  • Payout: When you win Hearts Jackpot for the second consecutive time, you receive BOTH the Hearts Jackpot AND the entire Super Jackpot!
  • Reset: After a Super Jackpot win, the Super Jackpot resets to zero, and you start fresh for the next consecutive win attempt.
  • Example: Win with 5♥ in round 1, then win again with 8♥ in round 2 = You win Hearts Jackpot + Super Jackpot!

🛡️ Insurance System

  • Who Can Buy: ONLY players with the SAME COLOR SUIT as the bank's suit can buy insurance!
    • If bank's suit is ♥ Hearts or ♦ Diamonds (red), only players with red suits can buy
    • If bank's suit is ♠ Spades or ♣ Clubs (black), only players with black suits can buy
  • Purchase: Buy insurance for $20 before each round (only if Insurance Pot ≥ $50). Insurance cost and minimum pot can be set in admin controls.
  • Insurance Pot: Receives 20% of all jackpot contributions and the insurance buy in from all players.
  • How to Win: Have the same rank AND suit as the bank's card (any suit, not just hearts)
  • Payout: Win the entire Insurance Pot (accumulates until won)
  • Example: If you have insurance and both you and the bank draw 5♠, you win the Insurance Pot!
  • Visual Indicator: Blue shield icon appears on player seat when insurance is purchased

🏆 Final Winners - Knock Out

  • Game End: The game ends when only 3 players remain or when players can no longer place bets
  • Top 3 Winners: The last 3 players with the highest stacks win prizes:
    • 1st Place (Highest Stack): Wins BOTH jackpots:
      • Hearts Jackpot: The entire accumulated Hearts Jackpot
      • Insurance Jackpot: The entire accumulated Insurance Jackpot
    • 2nd Place: Keeps their balance (chips they accumulated)
    • 3rd Place: Keeps their balance (chips they accumulated)
  • Goal: Stay in the game until the end and accumulate the highest stack to win both jackpots, or secure 2nd or 3rd place to keep your balance!

🎯 Special Rules

  • Turn Order: Default set to lowest balance first but can be changed in admin controls
  • Balance: Each player starts with $800 (can be adjusted in admin controls, range: $300-$2000)
  • Balance Conservation: Total money in game = Player balances + Hearts Jackpot + Insurance Jackpot + Bank Rake
  • No Rebuys: Players cannot add money during the game
  • Elimination: Players are eliminated when they cannot afford to place a bet

🎨 Card Colors

  • ♥ Hearts and ♦ Diamonds are red
  • ♠ Spades and ♣ Clubs are black

❓ Game Tutorial & Help

🔵 Blinking Blue Border (Current Turn Indicator)

When it's a player's turn to select a card, the player's seat will have a blinking blue border with a glowing effect.

  • What it means: It's this player's turn to select a card
  • Visual indicator: Intense blue blinking, glowing border and scaling effect
  • When it appears: During the card selection phase when the player should choose their card
  • For you as a player: When your seat (You) blinks blue, it's your turn to click on an available card

🎮 Player Strategy Buttons (Random, Lowest, Highest, Manual)

As a human player, you can choose between four different strategies for how to play:

📊 Strategy Options:

  • 🔄 Random: The game automatically selects a random available card for you. Perfect if you want to let the game run automatically.
  • ⬇️ Lowest: The game automatically selects the cheapest/lowest available card (A, 2, 3...). Safe strategy with low risk and low reward.
  • ⬆️ Highest: The game automatically selects the most expensive/highest available card (9, 8, 7...). High risk but big win potential.
  • 👆 Manual: You manually select the card by clicking on it. Full control over your choices!

How to change strategy: Click on your desired strategy button next to your player seat (to the left of "You" player) before or during the game.

🛡️ Insurance Button/Function

After all players have selected their cards, the insurance phase begins. Here, certain players can buy insurance to protect themselves.

⚠️ Important Insurance Rules:

  • Who can buy: ONLY players who have the SAME COLOR as the bank's suit can buy insurance!
  • Colors: Red (♥️ Hearts, ♦️ Diamonds) or Black (♣️ Clubs, ♠️ Spades)
  • Cost: $20 (default, can be changed in Admin Panel)
  • Minimum pot: The Insurance Jackpot must be at least $50 to be available
  • Win condition: If the bank draws the same number AND suit as your card, you win the Insurance Jackpot!
  • Visual indicator: Players who have purchased insurance will show a red marker at their player seat

How to buy insurance: If your player seat has the same color as the bank's suit and insurance is available, an insurance button will appear. Click it to purchase insurance.

🎛️ Human Player Insurance Settings:

As a human player, you can set your insurance behavior in the Bot Control Panel:

  • Always: Automatically buy insurance whenever available (if you have matching suit color)
  • Never: Never buy insurance automatically
  • Ask: Show a prompt asking if you want to buy insurance each time (default)

⚠️ Important: Insurance Jackpot Rules

If you win the Insurance Jackpot:

  • You WIN the entire Insurance Jackpot amount
  • However, normal game rules still apply - you LOSE your bet if your card matches the bank's number
  • The red marker at your player seat shows that you have purchased insurance
